Summary: this application does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It works entirely offline on your device. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and the application sends no information to us or to any third party.

1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect or receive any personal or sensitive user data. Specifically, the application does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, photos, files, usage statistics or crash logs.

2. Information stored on your device

The only information the application creates is the reminders you set yourself. For each reminder, the following is saved locally on your device by the Android notification scheduler:

  • the time of day and the weekdays you selected;
  • the title of the topic the reminder belongs to and the page number it opens.

This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is deleted when you remove the reminder in the application or uninstall the application.

3. Permissions and why they are requested

PermissionPurpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONSRequired by Android 13 and later to display the reminders you set.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARMSo a reminder appears at the minute you chose rather than whenever the system next batches background work.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDSo your reminders still work after the device is restarted.
VIBRATE, WAKE_LOCKTo alert you when a reminder is delivered.
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATEDeclared by the application framework and its components. The application makes no network requests: the book is bundled inside the application and is read from local storage.
ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY and other normal-level permissionsDeclared by the notification component so reminders respect your device's Do Not Disturb settings. These do not grant access to any personal data.

The application does not request access to your files, camera, microphone, contacts or location.

4. Third-party components

The application is built with React Native and uses open-source components to display the PDF book and to schedule local reminders. These components run entirely on your device and are not used to transmit data. The application contains no advertising networks, no analytics software development kits and no social network integrations.

5. Children

The application is not directed to children under 13. It does not collect any data, and therefore collects no data from children.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent or disclose any user data, because we do not collect any.

7. Data retention and deletion

As no data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. The reminders stored locally on your device are removed when you delete them in the application or uninstall it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
